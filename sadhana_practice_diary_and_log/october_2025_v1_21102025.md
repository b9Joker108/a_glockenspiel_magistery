# October 2025 #

## Tuesday, October 21, 2025 - xxx, October 1, 2025 ##

Well, I just went on my third bike ride in so many days on my new pushbike. The glockenspiel incited an image of me swimming in a natural body of water which cascaded into the purchase of a pushbike to realise this with rhythm and regularity in my lifepath. I have not yet gone for a swim in a natural body of water. It is only a matter of days now. The bike will be my vehicle to do so. I am currently in the process of the reclamation of manifestly personally important ever-so-little joys, that due to curiosities and happenstance in my lifepath have been deprecated and ellicited a soul-dampening lamentation as a result. This should be understood as a process of Soul Retrieval. As soon as I go for a swim, that is my cue to return and reintegrate with the glockenspiel as a daily interlude of joy, not practice. Practice, for me, smacks of \*icky yucky\*. So, I am not going to do glockenspiel 'practice', *in lieu* of Bhajana proper, we are going to have 'playtime'.
