## Tuesday, October 21, 2025 - xxx, October 1, 2025 ##

Well, I just went on my third bike ride in so many days on my new pushbike. Weeks and weeks ago, the glockenspiel incited an image of me swimming in a natural body of water in my visionary mind's eye, which cascaded into the purchase of a pushbike to realise this with rhythm and regularity in my lifepath. I have not yet gone for a swim in a natural body of water. It is only a matter of days now. The bike will be my vehicle to do so. I am currently in the process of the reclamation of manifestly important ever-so-little personal joys, that due to curiosities of happenstance arising in my lifepath, have been unduly and needlessly deprecated and in so doing have ellicited an insidious soul-dampening lamentation as a result. So, this process of reclamation of little joys, should be understood as paradigmatic of shamanic Soul Retrieval for lack of a better term. As soon as I go for a swim, that is my cue to return and reintegrate with the glockenspiel as a daily 'interlude of joy', NOT musical 'practice', which the eternal child in me detests. Musical 'practice', for me, smacks of \*icky yucky\*. So, I am not going to do glockenspiel 'practice' any more: *in lieu* of Bhajana proper, we are going to have glockenspiel and vocal 'playtime' to bootstrap Bhajana.
