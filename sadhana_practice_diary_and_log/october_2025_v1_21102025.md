# October 2025 #

## Tuesday, October 21, 2025 - xxx, October 1, 2025 ##

Well, I just went on my third bike ride in so many days on my new pushbike. Weeks and weeks ago, the glockenspiel incited an image of me swimming in a natural body of water in my visionary mind's eye, which cascaded into the purchase of a pushbike to realise this with rhythm and regularity in my lifepath. I have not yet gone for a swim in a natural body of water. It is only a matter of days now. The bike will be my vehicle to do so. I am currently in the process of the reclamation of manifestly important ever-so-little personally joys, that due to curiosities and happenstance in my lifepath have been deprecated and ellicited an insidious soul-dampening lamentation as a result. This should be understood as a process of shamanic Soul Retrieval for lack of a better term. As soon as I go for a swim, that is my cue to return and reintegrate with the glockenspiel as a daily 'interlude of joy', not 'practice', which the eternal child in me detests. 'Practice', for me, smacks of \*icky yucky\*. So, I am not going to do glockenspiel 'practice' any more, *in lieu* of Bhajana proper, we are going to have glockenspiel and vocal 'playtime'.
