### **What is "Karpur Gauram Karunavtaram"?**

**"Karpur Gauram Karunavtaram"** (also written as "Karpūra Gauram Karuṇāvatāram") is a revered Sanskrit hymn (stotra) dedicated to **Lord Shiva**. It is one of the most popular and widely recited Shiva mantras, known for its poetic beauty and profound spiritual meaning.

### **Text in Sanskrit (IAST Transliteration)**

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
कर्पूरगौरं करुणावतारं  
संसारसारं भुजगेन्द्रहारम्।  
सदा वसन्तं हृदयारविन्दे  
भवं भवानीसहितं नमामि॥

**IAST Transliteration:**
karpūra-gauraṃ karuṇā-avatāraṃ  
saṃsāra-sāraṃ bhujagendra-hāram  
sadā vasantaṃ hṛdayāravinde  
bhavaṃ bhavānī-sahitaṃ namāmi

### **Translation (Literal and Poetic)**

**Literal Translation:**
- *karpūra-gauram* — as white as camphor
- *karuṇā-avatāram* — the embodiment of compassion
- *saṃsāra-sāram* — the essence of worldly existence
- *bhujagendra-hāram* — adorned with the king of serpents (a snake garland)
- *sadā vasantaṃ hṛdayāravinde* — always residing in the lotus of the heart
- *bhavaṃ bhavānī-sahitaṃ namāmi* — I bow to Shiva, together with Bhavani (Parvati)

**Poetic Translation:**
"As white as camphor, the incarnation of compassion,  
The essence of the world, adorned with the king of serpents,  
Ever dwelling in the lotus of the heart,  
I bow to Shiva, together with Bhavani (Parvati)."

### **Context and Usage**

- This mantra is often recited during **Shiva worship (puja)**, especially during the evening aarti or at the conclusion of rituals.
- It is popular in both household and temple settings, and also used in yoga and meditation practices.
- The hymn is short, making it easy to memorize and recite, yet it encapsulates the essence of Shiva’s attributes: purity, compassion, transcendence, and unity with Shakti (Parvati).

### **Philosophical and Spiritual Significance**

- **Karpūra (Camphor):** Symbolizes purity and the dissolution of ego, as camphor burns away leaving no residue.
- **Gauram (White):** Represents spiritual purity and transcendence.
- **Karuṇāvatāram (Embodiment of Compassion):** Shiva is seen as the compassionate savior who liberates devotees from the cycle of birth and death.
- **Bhujagendra-hāram (Snake Garland):** The snake symbolizes Kundalini energy and mastery over fear and death.
- **Bhava and Bhavani:** The union of Shiva and Parvati signifies the inseparability of consciousness and energy, or the masculine and feminine divine principles.

### **Origins**

- The exact origin of this stotra is not clear, but it is often attributed to ancient Shaiva scriptures or oral tradition. It is sometimes included in the rituals of the **Rudra Abhishekam** and is found in various Shiva Puja manuals.
- It is not part of the major Vedic texts but has become an integral part of devotional Shaiva practice.

### **Cultural Impact**

- The hymn is set to music in countless renditions by classical and contemporary artists.
- It is often sung in Indian temples and at spiritual gatherings, and is beloved for its soothing, meditative quality.

### **Summary**

"Karpur Gauram Karunavtaram" is a timeless, deeply meaningful hymn to Lord Shiva, expressing the highest ideals of purity, compassion, and spiritual unity. Its recitation is believed to bring peace, clarity, and a sense of the divine presence in the heart of the devotee.
