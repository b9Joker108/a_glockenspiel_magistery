##

The inner numbering of 1 through 21, between 7 and 27
suggests the popular mahiShasuramardini stotra.

A portion of the stotra is also found in shivarahasya part 1, adhyAya 53/9-21

The stotra is incorrectly attributed to
Shankaracharya as is evident from its absense
from his complete works and as given in the
specialized link-references given in the end.
